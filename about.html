About

The Project

This is a music album tracker built for my Website Development 2 assignment.

It lets you browse, search, sort, add and delete albums from a collection of some of my favourite records.

Built with JavaScript, Handlebars templating, and localStorage for data persistence.

The Dataset

The collection spans shoegaze, indie rock, grunge, hip hop and progressive rock:

  • 8 preloaded albums including Panchiko, My Bloody Valentine, Pink Floyd and more
  • Properties: title, artist, genre, year, rating (1–10)
  • Fully searchable by title or artist
  • Sortable by any field, ascending or descending
  • Data saved in your browser via localStorage
